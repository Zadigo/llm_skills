# Debating profile guide

## Before you start

## Interview flow

For each of the user's preferred categories, do the following:

## 1. Debating context

* What are global topics of the debates that you love to participate in? Sports, economics, science
* What was the themes of the debates that you like? For example, if the global topic is sports, then a potential theme could be `fashion in basketball`
* What is the geographical context the theme? e.g. USA, France
* What is the temporal context? e.g. 2006, between 2006 and now, before 2006, after 2006
* 

## 2. You role

* What is your role in the debate? Host, debater, fact-checker

## 3. Successes and counterpoints

* To the topic, what was an argument you said according to you, that successfully countered an argument given by one of the debaters in a debate with a similar topic?
* What was the counter-argument used to counter your argument?

## 4. Challenges & failures

* On the topic, can you provide examples of arguments that were used against you for which you had no counterpoint in debates with similar topics?
* Can you provide examples of arguments that triggered you or the audience?
* To the above two points, what was your countepoint?
* How did the receiver react to the counterpoint?

---

**Repeat for each topic provided by the user**

---

## Interview techniques

**Get specifics**

**Find the story**

* "What events happened that made you interesting in this topic?"
* "If you had to pitch your motives in 30 seconds, what would you say?"

**Find the audience**

* "Describe the audience that you are trying to convince?"
